The idea

Three habits, kept every day

?

It asks why

What makes a move start, what sustains it, what kills it — which levers are pulled, and where capital goes when it leaves somewhere else.

✎

It writes the day down

Not only what the price did, but what the world looked like while it did it. Written once, in the moment, and kept permanently.

✓

It tests — and can say no

An answer becomes a claim that can come back negative: measured against chance, after costs, over the whole record. “No edge” counts as a result.

The part that matters most

Raw candles can be re-downloaded at any time.

The explanation of a day cannot.

Every day is recorded as a single image — the macro backdrop, the flows, the mood, what was known and what wasn't. A daily file weighs a few kilobytes; a century of them weighs less than one day of raw candles.

It makes a question possible that cannot be asked today:

“Find me the days whose macro image resembles today's — how did this mechanism behave then?”
