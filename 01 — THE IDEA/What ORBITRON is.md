# 🛰️ What ORBITRON is

A personal research project about **market mechanics** — what moves prices, and how
a trend is born, sustained and dies. On every scale: from twenty minutes to a
quarter. The first market studied is bitcoin.

---

## The sentence that explains the rest

> **We are looking for answers — and we write them down.**

The question is never *"what will the price do"*. It is **why did the trend move
in this direction, in this regime — and what was happening behind the scenes?**

And an answer that is not written down is a story. So every day is recorded as it
happened, permanently, building a history that did not exist before — the only thing
future answers can honestly be tested against.

This explains most of the decisions in this vault — why negative
results are recorded and kept, why a format is frozen before the logic that fills
it, why a validation engine is built to be able to say *no*.

---

## What it actually does

```
watch the market  →  notice something  →  ask what kind of day it is
      →  write the day down, permanently  →  test the answer against the record
```

Six steps, each with a name and a boundary. [The chain](../03%20%E2%80%94%20THE%20SYSTEM/The%20chain.md) walks through them.

---

## Bitcoin is the first asset, not the only one

The architecture keeps the collector and the core as the stable base, while the
research side stays deliberately fluid. Over time it may look at different windows,
different methods and different assets. The system is designed to be **re-tuned**,
not to follow one fixed model forever.

---

## Who runs it

One person — **[orbilob](https://github.com/orbilob)** — whose idea, direction and
supervision the whole thing runs on, with an AI agent (Claude Code) doing the
reading, drafting and implementation under written rules.

**The operator decides; the agent proposes and executes after approval.** Every
question in this vault is his question, and every decision recorded here is his
decision. That arrangement is documented in
[Working with an AI agent](../05%20%E2%80%94%20HOW%20WE%20WORK/Working%20with%20an%20AI%20agent.md), because it is a real part of how this project is
built and hiding it would make the rest less honest.

---

## What makes it worth reading

Not the results — there are none to boast about yet, and that is stated plainly
everywhere in this vault.

What is worth reading is the **discipline**: decisions recorded with a date and a
reason, evidence tagged as verified / claimed / unknown, a rule that one statement
lives in exactly one place, and a script that refuses a commit when the vault
contradicts itself.
